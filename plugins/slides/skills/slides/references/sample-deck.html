What we heard

Three Recurring Themes

Setup takes too long

Most stalled at the data import step and came back days later, if at all.

Teams join late

Admins wanted the workspace ready before inviting anyone, so invites waited.

Templates help

Those who started from a template finished setup twice as often.

Source: 40 interviews, coded by two researchers
